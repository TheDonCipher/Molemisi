# 33 — Molemisi Economy & Monetization Strategy

> **The simple edition.** Test: *could a newcomer understand the whole economy in one minute?*
> Molemisi is a cozy Botswana farm. This is the plan for how money works and what the store sells — written so the team, stakeholders, and any new player can hold it all in their head.
> **Status:** Living design reference · short on purpose · created 2026-10-01
> **Authority:** where this and `docs/MVP/` disagree, `docs/MVP/` wins — the differences are listed in §9.

---

## 0. The whole game in 30 seconds

**Two kinds of money and one smile meter.**

| Name | What it is | How you get it | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pula (P)** | Your farm money | Farming, selling, helping out | Seeds, water, animals, buildings, everyday decorations |
| **Madi (M)** | Real money — **1 Madi = 1 Botswana Pula** | With your phone: Orange Money, MyZaka, Smega | **Only** festival decorations and the Village Pass |
| **Botho** | A smile meter — how much your village likes you | Helping at the Kgotla (max 50 a day) | You don't spend it. It unlocks little helpers. |

**Two things cost real money:** decorations, and the Village Pass. That's all there is.

**One rule:** *never pay to win.* Money buys pretty things and saves a few taps — never crops, never land, never progress you can't earn by playing.

---

## 1. The cozy contract (design rules)

These are promises to the player. Every choice below has to keep them.

1. **Everything is reachable for free.** A player who never spends a thebe sees 100% of the content.
2. **Nothing dies and nothing is lost.** Crops wait. Animals get hungry, then recover. No game over, no restart.
3. **No pressure clocks.** No energy system, no "log in now or lose it", no countdown panic.
4. **Missing a day costs almost nothing.** Life happens; the farm waits.
5. **The store sells looks and time — never power.** No exclusive crops, no stronger animals, no Botho, no Pula.

---

## 2. What you can buy

Only **two** products. (The earlier plan had four revenue streams plus three boosts — we cut them. The boosts shipped broken, so they stay out until they actually work.)

### 2.1 Decorations — two shelves

| Shelf | Paid with | Prices | Who it's for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Market shelf** | **Pula** (earned) | **P200 / P600 / P1,500** | Everyone. This is also how Pula stops piling up forever. |
| **The Festival shelf** | **Madi** (bought) | **M40 / M80 / M150 / M300** | People who want the seasonal look. Completely optional. |

**Every Festival decoration has a Market-shelf cousin that does the same job visually.** Nobody's farm looks poorer because they didn't pay.

*Why decorations are the money-maker:* they're the only thing in the game with no cap on how many a person wants, they're pure art (which is what the studio needs to keep making anyway), and they never make anyone stronger.

### 2.2 Village Pass — M50 a month

| You get | |
| --- | --- |
| A helper that waters and collects while you're away | convenience, not power |
| One festival outfit each month | cosmetic |
| +50% storage space | convenience |

> The helper is **also unlocked free at Botho 500** — just later. Paying gets it early; playing gets it forever.

### 2.3 Buying Madi — top-up packs

**1 Madi = 1 BWP. Bigger packs add a flat 10% thank-you. That's the only math.**

| Pack | Pay | Get | |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Spark | **P5** | **5** | the first-purchase nudge |
| Farmer | **P20** | **20** | |
| **Harvest** | **P50** | **55** | the popular one — about one day of early-farm income |
| Cattle | **P100** | **110** | |
| Export | **P250** | **275** | top end |

- **Round amounts**, because that's how phone money actually works in Botswana. Odd prices (P47!) feel like a trick at the payment step.
- **Cap: P500 a day** (Botswana time) — a safety net against fraud, not a sales target. A typical player spends **P50–P75 a *month***.

### 2.4 Never for sale

No crops · no animals · no land · no water · no seeds · **no Pula** · **no Botho** · no season stamps · no gameplay advantage · no exclusive content.

## 3. Earning Pula

### 3.1 How much, by stage

| Stage | When | Pula a day | Spend it on |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| **Starter** | week 1 | **60–120** | Seeds, first animal |
| **Growing** | weeks 2–4 | **250–400** | Bigger field, workshop |
| **Settled** | month 2+ | **500–900** | Everything — save the rest for decorations |

**Comes from:** selling at the Co-op, the three daily Kgotla charges, contracts, a bit of foraging.
**Goes to:** seeds (the big one), water — then the next plot or animal.

### 3.2 Your first month

| Day | You… | Cost |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | First harvest | — |
| 2 | Get your first animal | P50 |
| 5 | Field grows to **8 plots** | P1,200 |
| 10 | Build the **Workshop** | P600 |
| 14 | Field grows to **12 plots** (target) | P6,000 |
| month 2 | Field grows to **16 plots** | P8,000 |
| month 3 | Field grows to **20 plots** — full farm | P15,000 |

The last two fields are **much cheaper than the old plan**, on purpose. The old final rung took **254 real days** to pay for itself — that isn't cozy, it's a reason to quit. Now every rung pays back in **about two months or less**.

### 3.3 You can't go broke

The cheapest seed is **P2** and a plot returns **P12**. Sell one harvest and you can replant four — even from zero. And there are always free ways to earn: forage in the Bushveld, run Kgotla errands. **Bankruptcy target: under 5% of players, realistically near zero.**

---

## 4. Botho — the smile meter

Fill it by helping the village. **Three charges a day** from the Kgotla is the main way; donations top it up. **Cap: 50 a day**, so a whale can't buy standing.

Three helpers unlock. Each one only **saves taps** — none of them grows more food:

| Botho | You unlock | |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| **100** | Bake bread | a new recipe |
| **300** | **Auto-feeder** | all your animals fed in one tap |
| **500** | **Auto-helper** | waters and harvests for you |

*Why the cap matters:* Botho decides who wins the monthly village prize — a real-money prize. If you could buy Botho, you could buy the prize. So you can't, and neither can anyone else.

---

## 5. Seasons — one rule each

The year is the **real Botswana year** — four seasons, three months each, the same for everyone. One simple rule per season:

| Season | Months | The rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🌧 **Rains** | Nov–Jan | Plant anything; water's cheap |
| 🌿 **Late rains** | Feb–Apr | Plant thirsty things now |
| ☀️ **Dry** | May–Jul | Hardy crops only; water costs more |
| 🌾 **Harvest** | Aug–Oct | Save up — festival time |

- **Six seeds are stocked each season.** That's what rotates your crops — not a level gate, not a paywall. Out-of-season seeds say *"available in the Dry season"* instead of vanishing, so you always know what you're waiting for.
- **Season stamps** mark which season you were here for. They're cosmetic, they reset every season, so spend them on that season's souvenir or they're gone. That little expiry is deliberate: it makes the season feel like an event instead of a chore.

---

## 6. What free players get

**All of it.** The only things money buys are looks and saved taps — and the saved taps are earnable too, at Botho 500.

The whole free path, in one line:

> plant what's in season → sell → buy the next field → add an animal → help the village → repeat.

Nothing in that sentence needs the store.

---

## 7. What the developer makes

Assumptions: **3% of players buy something each month** (cozy games beat the casual average), average spend **~P65/month**, running costs **~P10,000/month** (solo studio).

| Players | Buyers | Revenue / month | vs P10,000 costs |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 2,000 | 60 | ~P3,900 | not yet |
| **5,000** | 150 | ~P9,750 | **~break-even** |
| 10,000 | 300 | ~P19,500 | **+P9,500** |
| 25,000 | 750 | ~P48,750 | **+P38,750** |

- **Break-even ≈ 5,100 players** (P10,000 ÷ P65 ÷ 3%) — roughly 0.34% of Botswana's internet users.
- **No whales required.** High spenders are capped at 3% of buyers, and nothing in the game rewards a deep pocket over a loyal player.
- **Cheapest way to grow is the Kgotla itself.** We can't afford to buy installs, so Botho, the village prize and word of mouth do it. Community growth here is a *design feature*, not a marketing budget.

Guinea fowl ran at **-P2/day** on sorghum. | Feed them cheap sorghum; the goat gives 2 milk a day. | **SHIPPED** - now chicken **+P14**, fowl **+P7**, goat **+P21**, cow **+P27** a day. The bill is held and the period cut, which triples the daily drain and leaves the player annual cost unchanged. |

## 9. How this differs from the earlier plan

**Kept:** the Pula/Madi split · the Botho cap · the 5% market tax · the real four seasons · the "everything free" promise.

**Simplified or changed:**

| Earlier plan | Simple plan |
| --- | --- |
| 4 currencies | **2 money + 1 meter** (Chapter Tokens become season stamps) |
| 4 revenue streams + 3 boosts | **2 products**: decorations + a pass (boosts stay out until they work) |
| Sold Pula 1:1 for real money | **Pula is never sold** — simpler, and it makes "no pay-to-win" airtight |
| Final field P30,000 (254-day payback) | **P15,000** (~2-month payback) |
| Tiered pack bonus (up to +10% by tier) | **Flat 10% on big packs** — one sentence of math |

**One open question for the team:** the old spec also calls the *withdrawable marketplace* currency "Madi." If that marketplace ever ships, it needs a different name. Not urgent for v1.
